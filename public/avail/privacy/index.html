Avail Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Avail reads the calendars on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac to show your free time. Everything stays on that device. Vantum Works LLC does not collect, store, or transmit any of your data.

What Avail reads

With your permission, Avail uses Apple's EventKit framework to read the calendars and events on your device for the days shown on its canvas. It needs event times, whether an event is marked busy or free, and your response to an invitation, so it can tell busy time from free time. You choose which calendars count in Settings, and you can revoke access at any time: on iPhone and iPad in Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars, and on a Mac in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars.

What stays on your device

  • Your calendar data is read from the system calendar store each time Avail needs it and is not copied anywhere else.
  • Your preferences (time zones, the calendars you include, the number of days, display options) and any adjustments you make to individual events are stored locally in Avail's own app container.
  • The only data that leaves Avail is the message you send yourself: what Avail hands to the app you pick when you tap Share, or places on the clipboard when you press Copy. It contains dates, times, and time zones, never event titles, attendees, locations, or calendar names.

What Vantum Works does not do

  • Avail has no account, no sign-in, and no server of its own.
  • Avail does not connect to the internet, except that links in Settings open this website in your browser.
  • Avail contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting libraries, and no third-party SDKs.
  • Avail does not track you across apps or websites.

App Store

If you install Avail from the App Store or the Mac App Store, Apple handles the purchase and installation under Apple's privacy policy. Apple may share aggregate, anonymous statistics about downloads with us. We receive no information that identifies you.

Children

Avail is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Because Avail collects nothing, we expect changes to be rare.
