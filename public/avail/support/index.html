Getting started on iPhone and iPad

  • Allow calendar access. Avail asks the first time you open it. If you declined, open Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars › Avail and choose Full Access.
  • Pick the calendars that count. Tap the gear, then Calendars. Events on checked calendars count as busy; uncheck a calendar to leave its events out of your free time.
  • Set your hours. Drag the orange lines to set when your day starts and ends.
  • Open a day. Pinch into a day, or touch and hold its date and choose Open Day, to see your meetings. Pinch out to see all your days again.
  • Add a time zone. Tap the gear, then Time Zones, and search by city or country. Your free time is then shown in their hours too, and the message includes it.
  • Send your availability. Tap Share and pick Messages, Mail, or any other app. Or tap Copy and paste the message anywhere.
  • Take the tour again. Tap the gear, then Show Me Around.

Getting started on Mac

  • Allow calendar access. Avail asks on first launch. If you declined, open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars and turn Avail on.
  • Pick the calendars that count. Click the gear in the toolbar. Events on checked calendars count as busy; uncheck a calendar to leave its events out of your free time.
  • Add a time zone. Click the + beside the ruler and search by city or country. The same free time is then shown in that zone too, and the copied message includes it.
  • Copy your availability. Press Copy. Paste the message into chat, email, or a text.

Common questions

Why is an event not showing as busy?

Events marked Free in Calendar, invitations you declined, and events on calendars that are unchecked in Settings do not count as busy. Check the event's availability in Calendar or the calendar list in Avail's Settings.

Why does the day start or end at a certain hour?

The orange lines are your working hours. Drag them to change the hours Avail offers.

Can I move a meeting out of the way?

On iPhone and iPad, swipe a meeting to the right. On a Mac, click the events pill on a day and mark the event as movable. Either way, Avail counts that time as free in your message, without changing your calendar.

How do I undo a change on iPhone or iPad?

Shake your device, or swipe left with three fingers. Swipe right with three fingers to redo.

Does Avail send my calendar anywhere?

No. Avail reads the calendars on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac and keeps everything there. See the privacy policy.

Mac keyboard shortcuts

  • ⌘+ / ⌘− / ⌘0: zoom the canvas in, out, and back to actual size (or pinch on the trackpad)
  • ⌘] / ⌘[: show one more or one fewer day
  • Help › Show Me Around: take the tour again

Requirements
