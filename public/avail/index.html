Avail

The anti-link scheduling tool.

Avail reads your calendars, shows the gaps, and copies a table of your free time in any time zones you choose. Paste it into the email. Send that, not a booking link.

Download on the Mac App Store Download for Mac Coming soon to the Mac App Store Support

See your week as gaps

Every calendar you choose, drawn as day columns. Free time is what is left, and it is the point.

Any time zones

Add a colleague's zone to the ruler and the same free time reads in their hours, with their date.

Copy, paste, done

One click copies a clean table of your availability for email, chat, or a document.

Private by design

Avail reads calendars on your Mac and keeps everything there. No account, no server, nothing sent anywhere.

Requirements

macOS 26 or later. Version 1.0, notarized by Apple (3 MB). Calendar access is requested on first launch and can be changed any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars.
